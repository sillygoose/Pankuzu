# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 2026**

## Overview

Pankuzu is designed with your privacy in mind. All data collected by the app stays on your device or within your iCloud account.

## Data Collection

### Vehicle Data
Pankuzu collects vehicle telemetry data through your OBDLink adapter, including:
- Battery state of charge and energy levels
- Charging session information (power, energy added, duration)
- Vehicle identification number (VIN) for vehicle identification

This data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with any third parties.

### Location Data
With your permission, Pankuzu collects location data to:
- Record trip routes and display them on maps
- Calculate trip distances and elevation changes
- Provide context for your driving patterns

The app requests "Always" location permission to continue tracking trips when the app is in the background but location is only collected when a trip is active.

### Weather Data
Pankuzu retrieves weather information from Apple Weather services based on your location during trips. This is used to help you understand how weather conditions affect your vehicle's range and efficiency.

## Data Storage

All trip, charge, and vehicle data is stored on your device or in your iClooud account. You have full control over this data:
- View your complete trip and charge history
- Delete individual trips or charges
- Save and restore your data to JSON files

## Data Sharing

Pankuzu does not:
- Share your data with third parties
- Use any analytics or tracking services
- Display advertisements

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:
- **Location (Always)**: To track trip routes while driving, including when the app is in the background
- **Notifications**: To alert you about charging status and trip events
